# LeadQualifier — 60-second demo script
> **VISHAL'S STEP — do not have anyone else record this.** Record your own
> screen + voiceover (Loom or phone). ~60 seconds, three beats. Keep it
> conversational, not salesy.

**Setup before recording:** stack running (`docker compose up -d`), workflows imported
and Active, sample data seeded. Have three browser tabs ready: intake form
(`http://localhost:5000/`), mock Slack (`/mock-slack`), dashboard (`/dashboard`).

---

**[0:00–0:15] The pain + the intake**
*(Tab: intake form. Fill it live as a hot lead — founder, "leads sit in Gmail for
6 hours", budget $3k–$10k, timeline ASAP.)*

> "Say you're a realty owner. Every inquiry sits in Gmail for six hours before anyone
> replies — and you've lost listings over it. You fill in one form…"

**[0:15–0:40] The pipeline works**
*(Tab: mock Slack. Refresh — the HOT LEAD alert is there with score + one-line
rationale.)*

> "…and sixty seconds later, your senior rep gets this in Slack: score 88 out of 100,
> with the reason in one line. No one touched it. No spreadsheet."

**[0:40–0:55] The guardrail (the part buyers remember)**
*(Back to the form — submit a vague lead: no company, 'just exploring AI'.
Refresh mock Slack — junk auto-filed. Then show a 50–70 lead's Approve/Reject buttons.)*

> "Vague leads get filed automatically — zero rep time. And the borderline ones?
> They pause for a one-click approve. The system never guesses on your behalf."

**[0:55–1:00] The close**
*(Tab: dashboard.)*

> "Every lead, every score, every routing decision — on one dashboard. That's
> LeadQualifier."

---

**Don't say:** "AI-powered", "revolutionary", any metric you didn't measure.
**Do say:** what you see on screen, in plain words.
